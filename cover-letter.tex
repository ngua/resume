\textbf{Dear [Company Name] Team,}

I am writing to express my interest in a senior Rust-focused engineering role at [Company Name]. Over the past several years I have worked on distributed systems including industrial automation, SCADA, blockchain infrastructure, and production ML/LLM platforms. I would characterize the overall focus of my work as systems design, safe concurrency, and pragmatic engineering. I've helped design, build, and maintain infrastructure that must operate reliably under real-world constraints, including thousands of edge devices in critical energy infrastructure.

Most recently at PAKScada (formerly Plow Technologies, following acquisition), I have designed and implemented a production ML and LLM extension to our SCADA scripting language. This involved building a hybrid Haskell/Rust orchestration layer on AWS that handles model abstraction, runtime dispatch and script evaluation, EC2 lifecycle management, time-series database integration, permissions isolation, and secure communication between services. The system has now become essential to the workflow of some of our largest customers and has solved real-world constraints that previously limited the utility of our SCADA scripting language.

A large part of my work has involved designing systems that operate under strict reliability requirements. In addition to the work described above, I have helped re-architect and re-implement the package management system for edge devices. This includes coordinating package installations and updates across thousands of remote devices on unreliable cellular networks and resolving long-standing failure modes in low-connectivity environments.

I care deeply about correctness, type-driven design, and reproducibility. Nix/NixOS and functional programming practices have been central to my development and tooling philosophy, with a focus on reproducibility and determinism. I would consider myself equally pragmatic, however; for example, having helped introduce Rust into a predominantly Haskell codebase where it was the most appropriate solution.

Prior to this, at MLabs, I worked on blockchain infrastructure within the Cardano ecosystem, developing and reviewing smart contracts and serving as a technical liaison with IOHK/IOG. I co-designed and helped implement a PureScript interface layer for browser-native smart contract interaction. At MLabs, I was promoted to Delivery Manager within a year, where I coordinated distributed teams across various projects and managed relations with clients while overseeing the timely delivery of complex Cardano projects.

Although my academic background is quite diverse, including sociology, linguistics, and finance (CFA Level I; not currently a candidate for CFA Level II), the common thread has always been systems, i.e. how complex structures behave, fail, and can be modeled. I am largely self-directed in my technical development, and that path is the result of a sustained curiosity that continues to drive my work. I have an innate desire to solve problems, no matter the initial difficulty or complexity, and genuinely enjoy handling technical challenges.

Having lived and worked internationally, including in Vietnam, I am comfortable operating in diverse, distributed environments and value teams that are technically rigorous but collaborative and welcoming. I believe my international background has helped me in mentorship roles, especially at MLabs, where I was responsible for both communicating and collaborating with clients as well as overseeing the development and performance of my development teams. In short, I view the social dimension of software development to be as essential as its technical aspects, and appropriate and thoughtful communication is key to this.

I am particularly drawn to smaller engineering teams where thoughtful systems design, ownership, and technical integrity matter more than process overhead. I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to and learn from a team building serious infrastructure.
